The page of every trio is also a plain link on the home page, which works without JavaScript.

- 2026-11-18 Developer tooling (branch kit-checks): `python -m trioforge.checks.kits --results <folder>` scores the kits of a precompute output against checks of what a player would call obviously right (caster items on a melee or tank build, armour and robes on a tank wherever they are worn, one set everywhere, the bow of a Ranger, resists, the soft cap, the stamina parked under the stat knee and a few named trios) and prints how many kits pass or fail by role and tier, with the exchange rates of the named kits, so a change of the solver can be compared with the one before. It is a scoreboard, not a ranking of players' opinions and not a set of targets, and it changes nothing the site shows. The two gate checks fail by design today, so `--gate` is in neither the release build nor CI, and it refuses results that it cannot trust (`pipeline/trioforge/checks/`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, CONTRIBUTING).
- 2026-11-18 A trio that can use a bow can ask for one, and a trio with a Ranger has a role for it (branch ranged-role). The Weapons assumption of a trio page has a new choice, **Bow**, on every trio that has a Warrior, a Paladin, a Ranger, a Shadow Knight or a Rogue (the classes whose bows the catalog lists): the range place must hold a bow, the bow is picked for its own damage per delay, at the haste the kit has, times a new rule that starts at 1 (the damage bonus of a main hand is not counted for a bow, and neither is arrow damage), and the main hand and the off hand are picked for their stats alone. Bow is offered after the other choices in Balanced, Tank and Melee, whose best setup is still the one of the highest value, and it is the one choice of a new role, **Ranged** ("Damage from a bow first: attack, dexterity and haste, then hit points"), which a trio with a Ranger has after its other roles when the items it can use hold a bow that a class of the trio can shoot (a class needs the Archery skill, as the client's skill caps give it, as dual wield and the shield setup are gated). In the Bow setup the range place always holds a bow, even when damage is worth nothing, and when the Ranged role is chosen the notes that name the role weights name its rule beside the templates. Arrows are not counted: a kit has no place for them, and the page says so beside the range line, which shows the bow in the Bow setup. Two new rules, both judgments (the seal Model, with notes that say so): the Bow damage factor, 1, and the weights of the Ranged role (the Melee weights, whatever they are, with the dexterity weight doubled and the armor class weight halved). A trio with no class that can use a bow has the answers it had and a page that looks the same. A link to a kit made for the Bow setup says so (a small marker in the link: it carries the setup and nothing else new), and the page values such a kit under the Bow setup with its bow scored; a link with no marker is read as every link always was. The list of other weapon setups names the bow and the hands that go with it.
- 2026-11-18 Your priorities: a control next to Race, Role and Tier of a trio page sets how much each of eight stats counts for you (hit points, armor class, mana, attack, dexterity, haste, resists and endurance), in five steps: Off, Low, Normal, High and Top, which multiply the weight the role gives the stat by 0, 0.5, 1, 2 and 4. The kit is then worked out again in your browser with those weights (the optimizer is asked for, as it is for a slider), the page says in one line near the kit that it is your own, without runner-ups or robustness labels, with a Reset, and the address carries your steps (`p`), so a link shows the same kit; every step Normal is the page as it was, and every link made before is as it was. A button under the control says, on request, what one more point of stamina, strength, WIS and INT (the ones the trio casts with), armor class, dexterity, haste, maximum mana, endurance and a resist is worth on the kit shown, in the hit points the kit value is counted in (in the points of the build's score once a step is set), and the screen reader is told. A stat that the build does not count (haste for a caster, mana for a trio with no mana pool) is greyed out with a note, and with every step Off the page says that there is nothing to optimise: it keeps the kit it shows, and when the race, role, tier or weapons are changed it shows the build's own kit for them, with the steps left at Off. - 2026-11-22 Some rules now come from the game client's own calculations, and the Rules page says so. The planner no longer grows an item's attack with its upgrade tier: the client never does, so a +4 or a +10 item has the attack it has at +0. The armour class soft cap follows the game client: it is never below 350 before the Combat Stability and Physical Enhancement percent, and the part of the armour class past it counts at least 0.3, so a trio of casters is held to a higher soft cap (385 and not 360 at level 50, with Combat Stability 3 and no Physical Enhancement, which a trio of casters does not get unless the planner is told so) and gets more from armour past it. A Monk's worn-weight armour class and an Iksar's racial armour class now count inside the armour class that the soft cap limits, as the client does, so a Monk or an Iksar gets less from armour that is already past the cap. A resist now counts up to 1000 a school, the cap of the game's own stat window, where the planner stopped counting at 200. Dexterity now counts in full up to 510 (the planner valued a point past 255 at a tenth, and the game has no such break). A stat that is a cost on an item (a negative attribute or resist) now shrinks toward zero as the item is upgraded, as it does in the game, where the planner left it as it was. The cap on haste follows the level, as in the game (75 at level 50, and a higher cap at the levels after it): nothing changes at level 50. The Melee build of a trio with a Berserker now plays the Berserker stance: its weapons swing 2.1 times as fast after the haste cap, and agility gives no armor class or evasion; the Assumptions say so. A weapon with no listed damage bonus now gets the game's formula of the level: a two-hander at level 50 has 0.6875 of its delay (up to 50), where it had 0.67, and a weapon with more damage than the level counts its own damage. The trio page now gives both answers about haste, with the reason, beside the haste figure and the box "I have a haste spell": the best worn haste counts in full without a spell, and with the trio's spell (named) only the cap minus the spell counts, so a smaller haste item is enough and its slot goes to stats.
