# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The first milestone preview: the Paladin / Monk / Berserker page, with the best kit for each race, role and tier, and the rules, sources, about and changelog pages.
- Every page shows the data date and the game patch it was built from. Data releases are named `data-YYYY-MM-DD`; each one will be listed here with a link to its report of changed picks.
- Rewards of quests whose wiki page links to the page of a live zone under the name of the test, such as the Plane of Sky class tests, now count as a live source when the item has no other live source: 99 items that were hidden for lack of one are graded wiki.
- Every strict or wiki item that can be worn now has a page of its own (`/item/<key>/`): its stats at every tier from +0 to +10, the damage and delay of a weapon, where it comes from, how sure we are that it exists in EverQuest Legends, the trios whose default answer wears it and the credit to the wiki page it was read from. The names in the kit of a trio page and the items on the sources page link to them.
